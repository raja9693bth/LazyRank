# Security Policy

## Reporting Security Issues

At LazyProof, security, data integrity, and payment safety are top priorities. If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability, security flaw, or privacy issue, we appreciate your help in disclosing it responsibly.

### How to Report
Please report suspected vulnerabilities by emailing:
**support@lazyproof.online**

When reporting, please include:
- A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Detailed step-by-step reproduction instructions or a minimal proof of concept.
- The specific URLs, endpoints, or components involved.

### Reporting Guidelines
- **Do NOT** include real credentials, private tokens, passwords, or personal identifying information (PII) in reports.
- **Do NOT** engage in destructive actions, denial of service (DoS/DDoS), or unauthorized access to other users' data.
- Please allow reasonable time for the maintainers to review, investigate, and remediate the issue prior to public disclosure.
- Please note that LazyProof is maintained by an independent sole proprietorship; there is no unpromised monetary bug bounty or fixed contractual response deadline.
